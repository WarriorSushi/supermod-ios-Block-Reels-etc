Avoid invented social proof, diagnosis, shame, appearance insecurity, misleading fear claims, false urgency, or guaranteed time savings. The life grid is labeled an illustration, with age horizon and sleep assumptions. The 4.8-hour slider start follows the supplied reference; it is an editable starting estimate, not a claimed population measurement.

## In-app research cards

1. **PNAS Nexus (2025)**, “Blocking mobile internet on smartphones improves sustained attention, mental health, and subjective well-being.” https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf017 ; full paper https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11834938/
   - App wording: a randomized study found improved sustained attention after blocking mobile internet for two weeks.
   - Limitation displayed beside the claim: this was all mobile internet, not Supermod's selective filters. 119 of 467 met the study's compliance threshold.
   - No inferred Supermod efficacy percentage, no “10 years younger” marketing claim, no mental-health treatment claim.
2. **Hunt et al. (2018)**, “No More FOMO: Limiting Social Media Decreases Loneliness and Depression.” Journal of Social and Clinical Psychology, 37(10), 751–768. https://doi.org/10.1521/jscp.2018.37.10.751
   - 143 college students; Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat limited to 10 minutes each per day for three weeks versus usual use.
   - App wording: lower loneliness compared with usual use; small, short student study, not a test of Supermod; experiences vary.

Both cards have direct source links. Studies support their own interventions, not the effectiveness of this app. No fabricated endorsements or purchase claims.

## Interaction details

- First Continue switches Original to Supermod; second advances. Same for YouTube.
- Connections automatically visit feed, inbox, keyboard and profile. Continue can advance the demonstration without tapping the phone.
- Sponsored preview is a visibly labeled fictional coffee ad, removed and restored by the switch; Continue demonstrates removal before advancing.
- Full-resolution phone scene scales uniformly; bundled photographic sample avatars and content work offline.
- Life-grid finale uses 24 deterministic bouncing, stretching liquid drops and a wave pool, merged by Canvas blur/alpha threshold. It fills all safe areas in 3.7 seconds and then reveals the percentage. Continue cannot interrupt the fill. Reduce Motion renders the filled state without bouncing; animation stops rendering after settling.
- Launch storyboard and SwiftUI launch both use the same transparent mascot at 144 points, centered on the same named cream color. The former unmasked square launch asset is no longer referenced.
- Sample preview's caption is compact; screen size adapts to available height; editing button stays reachable.
